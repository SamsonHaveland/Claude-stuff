Coach-Lite

Open a match folder made by analyze.py. It should contain tracks.json, stats.json, the half videos, and coach.json once you've saved one.

Shortcuts: G our goal · A goal against · C chance · T teaching moment · Space play/pause · ←/→ ±5 s

Video not in folder:

    Press G our goal · A goal against · C chance · T teaching moment

    Click a dot to name the player. Give two tracks the same name to merge them; tracks break when kids bunch up.

    Player stats

    Distance and speed come from a single phone camera and are estimates. Kids far from the camera or in a crowd are tracked less reliably.
