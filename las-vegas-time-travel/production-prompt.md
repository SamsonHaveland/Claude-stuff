# THE MAKING OF LAS VEGAS — Production Prompt Package (v2)

Continuous time-travel tracking shot · 90 s · 16:9 · 4K
Revised from the v1 creative brief to fix historical, geographic and technical problems. Fact-checked as of October 2026.

## 1. What changed from v1, and why

|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The route and the chronology conflict.** A camera driving south at a steady pace passes the north Strip early, but much of what was built there came late (Wynn 2005, Encore 2008, Sphere 2023, Fontainebleau 2023). ## 4. Master prompt (paste into a long-form generator, or use as the bible for segmented generation)

> **THE MAKING OF LAS VEGAS — 125 years in one continuous shot.** Photorealistic, large-format cinema, 16:9, 4K. One continuous, stabilized forward tracking shot at human eye height (about 1.7 m), centred above Las Vegas Boulevard and looking straight south along the real route of the Las Vegas Strip. The camera glides forward slowly and steadily, never cuts, never reverses, never teleports. Time accelerates around it: roads pave themselves beneath the lens, buildings assemble floor by floor from real construction (cranes, steel, concrete, glass), neon signs flicker on, older resorts age, close and are imploded or demolished, and newer resorts rise on the same lots. Every landmark is historically accurate in design, date and real-world position: east side of the boulevard on the left of frame, west side on the right. No invented casinos, no fantasy architecture, no floating buildings, no dissolves.
>
> **1901–1940:** Silent, empty Mojave desert at warm morning light. Creosote and sagebrush, the Spring Mountains on the right horizon, Frenchman Mountain ahead-left. A wagon track appears, then a graded dirt road, then paved two-lane highway US-91 with wooden telephone poles and Model T, then 1930s cars. A small roadhouse, the Pair-o-Dice club, appears on the right.
> **1941–1959:** Golden afternoon. El Rancho Vegas rises on the right with its neon windmill, then the Last Frontier's western façade. On the left come the Thunderbird, the Sahara's camel-themed signage, the Riviera tower, the Desert Inn, the Sands, and far ahead the 1940s late-Moderne Flamingo. The Stardust's starburst sign ignites on the right. 1950s automobiles, young palm trees, the first neon reflections.
> **1960–1979:** Sunset into early night. Circus Circus's pink big-top appears on the right. Ahead on the right, Caesars Palace assembles its curved white tower, Roman statuary and fountains. Ahead on the left, the original 1973 MGM Grand tower. Giant roadside neon signs light up in sequence. Cars change through the 60s and 70s.
> **1980–2008:** Night. Time races. Ahead, the Mirage's gold-glass Y-tower rises and its volcano erupts. Excalibur's castle turrets, the black-glass Luxor pyramid with its vertical sky beam, the emerald MGM Grand, Treasure Island. The Dunes is imploded in a single cloud of dust and Bellagio rises behind a filling lake whose fountains burst. New York-New York's skyline and the Paris Eiffel Tower replica top out. The Sands is imploded and the Venetian's campanile rises on the left. Close on the left, the Desert Inn is demolished and the curved bronze towers of Wynn and Encore rise.
> **2009–2026:** Dense modern night. LED billboards replace neon, pedestrian bridges span the boulevard, traffic thickens. ARIA and CityCenter's glass towers and the Cosmopolitan rise ahead on the right. On the left, the High Roller wheel assembles and the Sphere lights up behind the Venetian with animated imagery. On the right, the Mirage goes dark, its volcano stops, and its tower is stripped and re-clad in blue glass beside a rising 42-storey guitar-shaped tower. Far ahead on the left, the Tropicana's towers disappear and stadium steel rises in their place.
> **2026, blue hour:** Time returns to normal speed. Passing Caesars Palace on the right and the Flamingo on the left, the camera reaches the Bellagio fountains as they erupt, and for the first time it slows. It rises smoothly while yawing 90° to the left to face east, until the entire Strip spans the frame from the STRAT tower on the left to Mandalay Bay on the right. The Sphere glows, the city grid spreads beyond, and the valley is ringed by dark mountains under a deep blue sky.
> **Title:** LAS VEGAS — 125 YEARS IN THE MAKING.
>
> **Lighting arc:** warm desert daylight → golden hour → neon dusk → saturated night → modern blue hour.
> **Sound arc:** desert wind → Model T engines → construction and jackhammers → 1950s lounge music bleed → electrical neon hum → implosion booms → dense traffic and casino ambience → orchestral crescendo that resolves to silence under the title.

## 5. Segmented build (how it can actually be produced)

No mainstream AI video model I know of can produce a 90-second, continuous 4K shot at this level of landmark accuracy in one pass. Most generate clips of roughly 5–25 seconds. The practical method is:

1. **Generate keyframes first.** Make one still per boundary below using an image model, with real reference photos of each era. Lock architecture, signage shapes and lighting at this stage.
2. **Generate each segment with first-frame and last-frame conditioning.** Each segment's end frame is the next segment's start frame, which keeps the motion continuous.
3. **Join the segments** with optical-flow matching at the boundaries, never a dissolve. Then upscale to 4K and add a single, unified colour grade.
4. **Lay one continuous sound bed** over the full assembly.

## 6. Risks to clear before release

- **Accuracy limit.** Generators approximate famous buildings and often get them wrong, especially pre-1970 casinos, whose reference imagery is mostly black-and-white. Plan on a human review pass for each segment and expect re-rolls. Compositing real 3D models (for example, period buildings built in Blender or Unreal) gives the most reliable accuracy, at higher cost.
- **Trademarks.** Casino names, logos, the Sphere's imagery and the Hard Rock guitar are trademarks. Fine for a personal or portfolio piece. For commercial or broadcast use, get a clearance review, or blur or genericise signage.
- **"Present day" goes stale.** The 2026 end state (Hard Rock and the ballpark under construction) will date quickly. Hard Rock targets Q4 2027 and the ballpark 2028. Update the finale if this is released later.
- **Not depicted (deliberately):** the 1980 MGM Grand fire and the 2017 Route 91 shooting site, to keep the tone celebratory. Revisit if the piece is meant to be documentary.
