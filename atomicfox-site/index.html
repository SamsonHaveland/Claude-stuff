    Lean or full crew, we build the team the job needs. No bloated packages and no unnecessary overhead. Every production dollar shows on screen.

Frequently asked

Common questions.

What types of projects does Atomic Fox Media produce?

Cinematic commercials, branded content campaigns and short films, and we support film and TV productions. Samson Haveland has been First AD on Apple TV+, Netflix, Lifetime and major studio features, and brings the same discipline to every Las Vegas commercial and brand project.

Do you work with agencies and out-of-state brands?

Yes. A significant share of our work is with agencies and brands coming into Las Vegas from out of state. We act as a full production partner, handling pre-production logistics, location scouting, crew, equipment and post-production handoff.

Are you a video production company or a full production partner?

Both. Samson has been First AD on MGM resort campaigns, LVCVA, Allegiant Stadium, Aria Sky Suites and Summerlin, and an independent producer through Atomic Fox and Commonwealth Productions. You get a partner who has run sets at every scale, from two-person shoots to major studio productions.

Can you help with local crews, permits and locations in Las Vegas?

Yes. We have deep relationships across the Las Vegas production community: local crew, location access (resort, architectural, desert, urban), permitting support and vendor coordination.

Do you handle branded content and commercials?

Commercial and branded content is a core part of what we do. We bring the same craft to brand work that we bring to narrative work. Your brand deserves the same discipline as a feature film.

Can you support film and TV development projects?

Yes. We develop original film and TV alongside our commercial work. If you're a producer or creative partner looking for production support on a scripted or unscripted project in Las Vegas, we're open to the conversation.

How do we start a project?

Use the form below or email hello@atomicfoxmedia.com. Tell us what you're making, your timeline and the scope you have in mind. We reply within one business day to set up a first conversation.
