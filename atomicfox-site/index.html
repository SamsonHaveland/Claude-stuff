Frequently asked questions.

What does Atomic Fox Media do?

Atomic Fox Media is a Las Vegas production company that makes commercials, music videos, branded content, short films and TV. We handle the whole job, from creative development and directing to crew, locations, permits and delivery, for local businesses and for agencies and brands shooting in Las Vegas.

Who runs Atomic Fox Media?

Atomic Fox Media is led by Samson Haveland, a producer and First Assistant Director with 25 years in film and TV and 46 credits on IMDb. His credits include Edge of Tomorrow, Maleficent, Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix, The Family Plan for Apple TV+ and Unsolved Mysteries for Netflix.

Has Atomic Fox Media won any awards?

Yes. The Anthem Injury Lawyers '24 spots Wake and Sleep won a Silver Telly Award in Local TV: Promotional at the 45th annual Telly Awards. Samson Haveland's short film Desert Grave won the Best Shorts Competition (Award of Merit, Special Mention), was a semi-finalist at eight festivals and was nominated at four more.

Where is Atomic Fox Media based, and where do you shoot?

We're based in Las Vegas, Nevada. We shoot across the Las Vegas Valley, from resorts and the Strip to modern interiors and the Mojave Desert, and we support productions coming in from out of state.

Do you work with agencies and out-of-state brands?

Yes. We act as a full production partner for agencies and brands filming in Las Vegas: pre-production, scouting, crew, equipment, permits and post-production handoff.

Can you handle crews, permits and locations in Las Vegas?

Yes. We provide local crew, permitting support and vendor coordination, and access to resort, architectural, desert and urban locations. Verified Las Vegas film locations are listed on FilmLV.com.

Is any idea too small, too big or too unusual?

No. We've worked on everything from two-person shoots to studio features. Tell us the idea and we'll tell you honestly how we'd make it.

How do I start a project with Atomic Fox Media?

Use the Pitch us builder or the contact form on this page, email samson@filmlv.com, or call 917-375-6736. We reply within one business day.
