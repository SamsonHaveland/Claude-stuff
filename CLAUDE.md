## Advisor use
Default to doing work yourself. Call the `advisor` subagent only:
- Before choosing an architecture or strategy for anything non-trivial
- After two failed attempts at the same problem
- Before finalizing high-stakes output (contracts, deploys, anything hard to undo)
Never call it for routine edits, lookups, formatting, or first drafts.
When calling: send a brief of 5-10 lines (goal, what's tried, specific question) and request a reply under 150 words. Do not paste transcripts or full files.
After: act on the conclusion; don't relay its reasoning back to me.
